Final Verdict

So Which One Is Better?

On accuracy: Jev won this test, clearly

Jev got 49 out of 56 right (88%). Laya got 41 out of 56 (73%). The gap was biggest on the "25 similar categories" test, where Jev went 8/8 and Laya only 4/8 — matching a weakness Laya's own documentation openly admits to. Jev was also noticeably better at urgency scoring.

But it's not a clean sweep. Laya resisted one trick question Jev fell for, tied Jev on picking the right specialist for a question, and — importantly — Laya is completely free and runs on your own computer, while Jev costs a small amount per question and requires an internet connection to TypeSafe's servers.

The fairest summary: if you need the highest accuracy out of the box, especially with many similar categories to choose from, Jev is currently ahead. If you want something free, open-source, and you're willing to fine-tune it or set it up properly (with its multilingual routing enabled, unlike this free demo test), Laya is a real and much cheaper alternative — just not quite as accurate as-is in this comparison.

Jev tested via direct calls to TypeSafe's API (typesafe/jev-1.13) through OpenRouter, paid, $0.003 total cost.
Laya tested via its free public demo (convaiinnovations/laya-demo on Hugging Face Spaces), English-only checkpoint, no cost.
